Welcome

The charm of Carantec, with the sea as backdrop

Tucked away at the end of a peaceful lane, the Vue Mer house offers an ever-changing view over the Bay of Morlaix that shifts with the tides. Here, time slows down: breakfast facing the water, a swim in the heated pool, walks along the customs officers' path just minutes away on foot.

Sea view from the bed

Wake up facing the Bay of Morlaix, with a view that changes with the tides and the light of day.

Heated pool

Prime location

Two ways to stay

The whole villa, or simply a room

Looking for a cosy spot for two with breakfast included? Book our sea-view room. Travelling as a group, with family or friends? Have our villa entirely to yourselves, heated pool included.

Renting our villa

The whole house just for you: 3 to 4 bedrooms each with its own bathroom, a spacious living room opening onto the sea, a large terrace and a 4x8-metre heated pool, available for weekly rental.
